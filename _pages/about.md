## About Me

Hello! I am **Yayun Yang**, an M.S. student in Statistics at the **University of Minnesota, Twin Cities**.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **statistical learning, optimization, and artificial intelligence**, with a particular focus on data-driven decision-making under uncertainty.

I am interested in developing statistical and computational methods for learning from complex, heterogeneous, and incomplete data, and using these methods to support reliable decisions in real-world systems.

My research spans applications in **energy systems, transportation, and biomedical data**, combining statistical inference, machine learning, and mathematical optimization.

I am planning to apply for **Ph.D. programs starting in Fall 2027**.

## Research Interests

My current research interests include:

- **Statistical Learning and AI:** Generative models, multimodal learning, representation learning, and uncertainty quantification.
- **Data-Driven Optimization:** Distributionally robust optimization, stochastic optimization, and decision-making under uncertainty.
- **Energy and Transportation Systems:** Power systems optimization, and energy market design.
